01 / THE PERSON

A different perspective comes from many disciplines.

My work sits at the intersection of scientific discovery, advanced engineering and the systems that help research thrive.

I hold a PhD in Electrical Engineering from Université de Sherbrooke, with earlier training in biomedical electronics, medical instrumentation and microbiology. That combination has shaped my research in molecular communication, bio-inspired devices, optogenetics and biomedical technology.

Today, as a Programs Officer at the Canada Foundation for Innovation (CFI), I also work on research-program processes, reviewer intelligence and the thoughtful use of AI and automation. My academic research and professional practice share the same guiding idea: complex challenges benefit from clearer systems and interdisciplinary thinking.
